About

Engineering profile

I'm a mechatronics engineer passionate in automotive, mechanical desing, product development, and engineering simulation — turning early ideas into geometry that can actually be built, tested, and trusted.

My work usually starts with a real life problem, or an improvement idea and ends with a model that has been checked against it, not just designed to look right. I'm most interested in the middle part of that process: the iteration between modeling a part, simulating how it behaves, and adjusting the design based on what the simulation actually shows.

I care about documentation and traceability as much as the model itself — a good CAD file should tell the next engineer why a decision was made, not just what the final geometry is.

Good design isn't the model that looks finished — it's the one that survives contact with a load case, a tolerance stack, and a manufacturing process.
